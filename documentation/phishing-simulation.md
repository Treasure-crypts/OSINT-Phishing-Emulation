# Phishing Email Simulation

## Objective
To understand the structure of a phishing email and identify
suspicious indicators using a fictional HR notification.

## Simulation Details
- Sender: hr@abc-technologies.example
- Recipient: employee@abc-technologies.example
- Reply-To: support@unknown-sender.example
- Subject: Urgent: Employee Policy Update

## Suspicious Indicators

1. Sender and Reply-To mismatch:
   The Reply-To address belongs to a different domain.

2. Urgent subject:
   The subject uses urgency to encourage quick action.

3. Sender verification:
   The sender's identity should be verified before trusting
   the message.

## SOC Analyst Observation
The Reply-To mismatch and urgency are suspicious indicators.
They require further investigation but do not independently
confirm that the email is malicious.

## Safety Note
This is a fictional, offline email simulation.
No email was delivered and no credentials were collected.
